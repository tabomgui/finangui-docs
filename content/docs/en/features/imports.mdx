**Importar extrato** (Import statement) imports a statement file. Nothing saves until you confirm a preview.

![The import statement screen](/screenshots/imports.png)

## Supported formats

<Tabs items={['Inter', 'Nubank', 'C6', 'OFX']}>
<Tab value="Inter">
CSV export of an Inter statement.
</Tab>
<Tab value="Nubank">
Two formats: **Nubank (conta)** (checking) and **Nubank (cartão)** (card).
</Tab>
<Tab value="C6">
CSV export of a C6 statement.
</Tab>
<Tab value="OFX">
A generic OFX file, for other banks.
</Tab>
</Tabs>

## Uploading a file

<Steps>
<Step>
Export the statement from your bank (`.csv`, `.ofx` or `.txt`, up to 2 MB).
</Step>
<Step>
Pick the **Conta** (Account) it belongs to. **Detectar automaticamente** (detect automatically) picks the format.
</Step>
<Step>
Upload the file: the **Prévia da importação** (Import preview) opens.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Preview before anything is saved

![Preview with imported rows](/screenshots/imports-preview.png)

- **Nova** (New) — not seen before.
- **Já importada** (Already imported) — locked in, can't deselect.
- **Atualiza** (Updates) — refreshes an existing entry.
- **Substitui parcela prevista** (Replaces projected installment) — real replaces projected.
- **Casa com lançamento manual** (Matches manual entry) — adopts your manual entry.
- **Confirma pendente** (Confirms pending) — replaces an unconfirmed entry.
Each row is checked by default. Unparseable lines show separately, with the reason.

## Confirming

- The button shows how many rows are checked.
- Matching is re-evaluated from scratch and the batch commits whole or not at all.
- **Cancelar** (Cancel) discards the batch.

## Reverting an import

- Only the latest completed import per account can be reverted.
- **Reverter** (Revert), on **Importações recentes** (Recent imports), deletes created entries and restores matched ones.

<Callout type="warn">
  A manual edit to a touched field is overwritten too.
</Callout>

## Categorization and transfers

- Same automatic categorization as manual entries (rule, then history). See [Categorization](/docs/features/categorization).
- Transfer detection runs after confirmation. See [Transfers](/docs/features/transfers).

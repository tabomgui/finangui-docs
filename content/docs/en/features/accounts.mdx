---
title: Accounts
description: Checking, savings, cash and credit card accounts, and how finangui computes the balance you see on each one.
---

An account in finangui is anything money sits in or moves through: a bank **Conta corrente** (checking account), a **Poupança** (savings account), **Dinheiro** (cash you hold) or a **Cartão de crédito** (credit card). Every entry you log or import belongs to one account, and every account's balance is always computed by the backend from its opening balance plus its entries — never typed in by hand and never calculated in the frontend.

Accounts live under **Contas** (Accounts), reached from the main navigation. A manual account (not linked to a bank) can be created, edited, archived or deleted there; an account linked through [Banking](/docs/features/banking) appears grouped under its connection instead, and can still be edited (name, color, icon, card fields), just not disconnected from that screen.

## How the balance is computed

An account's balance is **opening balance plus every non-ignored posted entry up to a given day** — by default, today, in the app's timezone. This is true everywhere a balance shows up: the **Início** (Home) dashboard, the accounts list, goal progress and the bank connection cards all use today's balance.

That "up to today" part matters for entries dated in the future: if you log a transaction dated tomorrow or later, it already exists and shows up in the account's history, but it doesn't move the balance you see until that date actually arrives.

<Callout type="info">
  A bank-connected account works differently from this default rule — see below.
</Callout>

## Bank-connected accounts: the balance anchors to the bank

For an account linked through **Banking** (not a credit card), the balance shown isn't just opening balance plus entries. Instead, it anchors to the balance the bank itself reported at the last sync, and only adds the difference from entries dated after that sync day.

<Callout type="info">
  This means a manual entry you add with a date on or before the last sync day won't move the account's balance — not until the next sync happens. The bank's own number already "won" for that period: finangui assumes the bank already accounted for everything up to the day it last reported a balance, so adding an entry inside that window wouldn't just double it, it would get silently absorbed at the next sync anyway. Entries dated after the sync day do move the balance normally.
</Callout>

Because of this anchoring, the balance shown for a connected account can occasionally look out of step with what you'd expect from just reading the entry list. For that case, finangui shows a secondary line next to the balance, labeled **Pelos lançamentos** (By the entries): this is the balance as computed purely from your own ledger, always as of today, with no anchoring to the bank. It's informational only — a way to compare and spot a divergence — and it is never the number used as the account's real balance anywhere else in the app.

## Credit card accounts: always the full ledger

A **Cartão de crédito** (credit card) account doesn't use the "up to today" cutoff at all: its balance is always the full historical ledger — opening balance plus every non-ignored posted entry ever recorded on it, with no date limit. This reflects that a credit card balance is really a running debt, not a point-in-time snapshot.

Statement (invoice) totals, due dates, payments and limit usage are a separate layer on top of the account balance, covered on the [Cards](/docs/features/cards) page.

## Credit card debt isn't in the Início total

The total balance shown on the **Início** (Home) dashboard adds up all your non-card accounts; credit card accounts are excluded from it. Card debt is shown separately, on the Cards ("Faturas") card of the dashboard, instead of being netted into your overall balance.

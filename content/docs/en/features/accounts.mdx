An account holds or moves money:

- **Conta corrente** (checking)
- **Poupança** (savings)
- **Dinheiro** (cash)
- **Cartão de crédito** (credit card)

Every entry belongs to one account. Balance: always computed by the backend, never typed in.

Accounts live under **Contas** (Accounts).

- Manual: create, edit, archive or delete here.
- [Banking](/docs/features/banking)-linked: grouped under its connection; name/color/icon/card fields editable; disconnect only from Banking.

## How the balance is computed

Balance = opening balance + every non-ignored posted entry up to today (app timezone).

- Used everywhere: Início, accounts list, goal progress, connection cards.
- Future-dated entry: shows in history, doesn't move the balance yet.

<Callout type="info">
  Bank-connected accounts follow a different rule below.
</Callout>

## Bank-connected accounts anchor to the bank

For a non-card account linked through **Banking**:

- Anchors to the bank's last-synced balance, plus entries dated after that sync day.
- Manual entry on or before the last sync: waits for the next sync.
- **Pelos lançamentos** (By the entries): ledger-only balance as of today, for comparison only — never the real balance.

## Credit card accounts always use the full ledger

A **Cartão de crédito** has no "as of today" cutoff — balance is always the full historical ledger. Statement details: [Cards](/docs/features/cards).

## Credit card debt isn't in the Início total

Início's total balance sums non-card accounts only. Card debt shows separately, on the Cards ("Faturas") card.

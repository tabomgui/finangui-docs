title: Accounts
description: Checking, savings, cash and credit card accounts, and how finangui computes the balance you see on each one.
---

An account in finangui is anything money sits in or moves through: a **Conta corrente** (checking account), **Poupança** (savings account), **Dinheiro** (cash) or **Cartão de crédito** (credit card). Every entry belongs to one account, and every balance is computed by the backend from opening balance plus entries — never typed in, never calculated in the frontend.

Accounts live under **Contas** (Accounts). A manual account can be created, edited, archived or deleted there; an account linked through [Banking](/docs/features/banking) appears grouped under its connection, and can still be edited (name, color, icon, card fields), just not disconnected from that screen.

## How the balance is computed

An account's balance is opening balance plus every non-ignored posted entry up to today (app timezone) — used everywhere a balance shows up: Início, the accounts list, goal progress and connection cards. An entry dated in the future already shows in the account's history, but doesn't move the balance until that date arrives.

<Callout type="info">
  A bank-connected account follows a different rule — see below.
</Callout>

## Bank-connected accounts anchor to the bank

For an account linked through **Banking** (not a credit card), the balance anchors to what the bank reported at the last sync, adding only entries dated after that sync day. A manual entry dated on or before the last sync won't move the balance until the next sync runs. finangui also shows **Pelos lançamentos** (By the entries) next to it — the balance computed purely from your own ledger, as of today — for comparison only; it's never the number used as the account's real balance.

## Credit card accounts always use the full ledger

A **Cartão de crédito** account has no "up to today" cutoff: its balance is always the full historical ledger, since a card balance is a running debt, not a snapshot. Statement totals, due dates, payments and limit usage are covered on the [Cards](/docs/features/cards) page.

## Credit card debt isn't in the Início total

The Início total balance adds up all non-card accounts; card debt is shown separately, on the Cards ("Faturas") card, instead of netted into it.

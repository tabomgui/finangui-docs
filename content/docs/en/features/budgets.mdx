---
title: Budgets
description: How finangui tracks a monthly spending limit per expense category, with one-off monthly exceptions and parent/child categories.
---

A budget is a monthly spending limit you set on a category, so you can see at a glance how much you've already spent against it. On the **Orçamento** (Budget) screen, **Orçar categoria** (Budget a category) opens a form to set one up — only for a non-archived expense category, never income or transfer.

![The Orçamento screen](/screenshots/budgets.png)

## Default and monthly exceptions

The amount you set becomes the default, applying to every month unless that month has its own exception. The **Vale para** (Applies to) toggle lets you override the default for just the month you're viewing (e.g. **Só outubro**); a category with an active exception shows a **Só este mês** (Only this month) badge.

<Callout type="info">
  Choosing **Sem orçamento só neste mês** (No budget just this month) instead of an amount cancels the budget for that one month only, without deleting the default or the exception itself.
</Callout>

## Parent and child categories

Budgeting a parent covers it and all its subcategories — a child's spending counts toward the parent's limit even if the child was never budgeted itself, and a child can still have its own limit on top. The totals at the top of the screen never double-count a budgeted child's spending.

## Spent, remaining, and progress

Each row shows a progress bar and percentage against its limit. Spending is net (expenses minus refunds) and never negative; past the limit, the "restam" (remaining) label switches to "excedeu" (exceeded). Spending in unbudgeted categories appears separately as **Fora do orçamento** (Outside the budget).

title: Budgets

A budget is a monthly spending limit on a category — expense categories only, never income, transfer or archived.

![The Orçamento screen](/screenshots/budgets.png)

## Budgeting a category

<Steps>
<Step>
On **Orçamento** (Budget), click **Orçar categoria** (Budget a category).
</Step>
<Step>
Pick the **Categoria** (Category) and the **Valor** (Amount). **Vale para** (Applies to) sets every month or just the month you're viewing.

![The Orçar categoria dialog](/screenshots/budgets-new.png)
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Salvar** (Save).
</Step>
</Steps>

## Default and exceptions

- The set amount is the default for every month.
- **Vale para** overrides just the month you're viewing.
- An active exception shows a **Só este mês** (Only this month) badge.

<Callout type="info">
  **Sem orçamento só neste mês** cancels one month only — the default and exception stay.
</Callout>

## Parent and child categories

- A parent's budget covers it and all subcategories.
- A child's spending counts toward the parent even if unbudgeted.
- A child can still have its own limit too.
- Totals never double-count a budgeted child.

## Spent, remaining, progress

- Each row: a progress bar and percentage.
- Spending is net (expenses minus refunds), never negative.
- Past the limit, "restam" switches to "excedeu".
- Unbudgeted spending shows separately as **Fora do orçamento**.

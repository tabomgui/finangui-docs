---
title: Banking
description: Connecting a bank account through Pluggy, what a sync does and doesn't touch, and how to read sync history.
---

**Banking** connects a real bank account to finangui through [Pluggy](https://pluggy.ai), an Open Finance aggregator, so transactions show up automatically instead of being imported by hand. Once connected, an account works like any other [account](/docs/features/accounts) — it just stays up to date on its own.

## Registering your own Pluggy credentials

Before connecting a bank, register your own Pluggy application credentials in **Configurações** (Settings), under **Integração bancária (Pluggy)** (Bank integration). finangui doesn't ship a shared provider — each user creates their own application on Pluggy's dashboard and enters its Client ID and Client Secret here; the secret is never shown again. Saving tests the credentials first. Without working credentials, banking features are turned off everywhere else in the app.

## Connecting an account

From **Contas** (Accounts), **Conectar banco** (Connect bank) opens Pluggy's widget to log in and pick which accounts to bring in. Each account is linked to a new account or, if it matches, to an existing manual one — so history isn't lost or duplicated.

## What a sync does

A sync brings in new transactions through the same pipeline as a file [import](/docs/features/imports) — same duplicate detection, installment matching, and automatic categorization; a provider-supplied category is only used as a last resort.

<Callout type="info">
  Every sync also revisits roughly the last 40 days already fetched, to catch a status change, a description update, or an invoice reassignment — never overwriting date, amount, category, or an invoice you picked by hand.
</Callout>

## Needing reauthorization

A connection can occasionally need you to log in again at the bank's side. finangui calls this status **Reconectar** (Reconnect) and surfaces an actionable banner, notifying you right away rather than waiting for the daily digest.

## Sync history

Each connection has a **Histórico** (History) screen listing every sync — trigger, outcome, and how many transactions were added or updated — with a detail view of the transactions added. **Sincronizar agora** (Sync now) triggers a sync on demand.

## Disconnecting

Disconnecting doesn't delete anything: accounts become manual again, keeping their full history, and just stop updating on their own.

## Direction is always read from the bank

A transaction's direction (in or out) comes from the type the bank reports, never from the sign of the amount — important because a credit card's amounts can be inverted.

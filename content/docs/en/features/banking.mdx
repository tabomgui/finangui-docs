title: Banking
description: Connecting a bank account through Pluggy, what a sync does and doesn't touch, and how to read sync history.

**Banking** links a bank account via [Pluggy](https://pluggy.ai), an Open Finance aggregator — it then works like any other [account](/docs/features/accounts), self-updating.

## Registering your own Pluggy credentials

- Register Pluggy app credentials in **Configurações** (Settings) → **Integração bancária (Pluggy)** (Bank integration).
- No shared provider: each user creates their own Pluggy app.
- Client ID and Client Secret; the secret is never shown again.
- Saving tests the credentials. Without them, banking stays off everywhere.

## Connecting an account

- From **Contas** (Accounts), **Conectar banco** (Connect bank) opens Pluggy's widget.
- Log in, pick accounts; each links to a new or matching manual one.

## What a sync does

Same pipeline as a file [import](/docs/features/imports): dedup, installment matching, categorization. Provider category is only a last resort.

<Callout type="info">
  Every sync revisits the last ~40 days for status, description, or invoice changes — never overwriting date, amount, category, or a chosen invoice.
</Callout>

## Needing reauthorization

A connection can need re-login at the bank. finangui calls this **Reconectar** (Reconnect) and notifies you right away.

## Sync history

Each connection has a **Histórico** (History) screen: every sync, trigger, outcome, counts added/updated. **Sincronizar agora** (Sync now) triggers one on demand.

## Disconnecting

Deletes nothing: accounts go manual again, keep their history, stop self-updating.

## Direction is always read from the bank

Direction (in or out) comes from the bank's type, never the amount's sign — a credit card's sign can invert.

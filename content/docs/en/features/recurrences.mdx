A recurrence is a repeating bill or income — rent, salary, subscription. Set up once; finangui projects it on the calendar.

![The Recorrências screen](/screenshots/recurrences.png)

## Creating a recurrence

<Tabs items={['From scratch', 'From a transaction']}>
<Tab value="From scratch">
**Recorrências** (Recurrences) → **Nova recorrência** (New recurrence): account, description, amount, category, direction, schedule. Optional matching text helps recognize it later.
</Tab>
<Tab value="From a transaction">
**Repetir** (Repeat), on a new entry, creates one — just asks the frequency.
</Tab>
</Tabs>

## Projected occurrences and matching

- Generated daily, and right on create/edit.
- Never duplicates a date, nor projects far past (or, on a card, before today).
- A real entry matches a projected one by amount and date, confirming it instead of duplicating.

## Overdue occurrences

An unmatched, past-due occurrence is overdue — see **Pendências** (Pending items) at Início:

- **Aconteceu** (It happened) — confirms it, details adjustable.
- **Não aconteceu** (Didn't happen) — skips it.

## Editing and skipping

- Simple field changes apply only to today-or-later occurrences.
- Changing the schedule, or pausing, regenerates recent occurrences.
- Marking **Não aconteceu** skips that date.

<Callout type="info">
  A skipped date is remembered forever — through a later schedule change, pause, or reactivation.
</Callout>

## Categorization

Excluded from categorization until confirmed as a real entry — see [Categorization](/docs/features/categorization).

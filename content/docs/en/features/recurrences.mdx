A recurrence is a bill or income that repeats — rent, a salary, a subscription. Set it up once; finangui projects it on the calendar.

![The Recorrências screen](/screenshots/recurrences.png)

## Creating a recurrence

<Tabs items={['From scratch', 'From a transaction']}>
<Tab value="From scratch">
**Recorrências** (Recurrences) → **Nova recorrência** (New recurrence): account, description, amount, category, direction, schedule. An optional matching-text field helps recognize the real entry later.
</Tab>
<Tab value="From a transaction">
The **Repetir** (Repeat) toggle, when logging a new entry, creates a recurrence from it — just asks the frequency.
</Tab>
</Tabs>

## Projected occurrences and matching

- Generated automatically, daily and right on create/edit.
- Never duplicates a date or projects far into the past (or, on a credit card, before today).
- A real entry matches a projected one by amount and nearby date, confirming it instead of duplicating.

## Overdue occurrences

A projected occurrence past due with no match shows as overdue, on the **Pendências** (Pending items) card on Início:

- **Aconteceu** (It happened) — confirms it, details adjustable.
- **Não aconteceu** (Didn't happen) — skips it.

## Editing and skipping

- Simple field changes apply only to occurrences dated today or later.
- Changing the schedule, or pausing, clears recent occurrences and regenerates them.
- Marking **Não aconteceu** skips that date.

<Callout type="info">
  A skipped date is remembered forever, even through a later schedule change, pause, or reactivation.
</Callout>

## Categorization

Excluded from automatic categorization until confirmed as a real entry — see [Categorization](/docs/features/categorization).

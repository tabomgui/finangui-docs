A recurrence is a bill or income that repeats on a schedule — rent, a salary, a subscription. Set it up once and finangui projects upcoming occurrences on the calendar automatically.

![The Recorrências screen](/screenshots/recurrences.png)

## Creating a recurrence

<Tabs items={['From scratch', 'From a transaction']}>
<Tab value="From scratch">
On the **Recorrências** (Recurrences) screen, **Nova recorrência** (New recurrence) opens a form for account, description, amount, category, direction, and schedule. An optional matching-text field helps finangui recognize the real bank entry later.
</Tab>
<Tab value="From a transaction">
The **Repetir** (Repeat) toggle, while logging a brand-new entry, creates a recurrence from it, asking only for the frequency.
</Tab>
</Tabs>

## Projected occurrences and matching

finangui generates upcoming projected occurrences automatically — daily, and right away on create/edit — never duplicating a date, and never projecting far into the past (or, on a credit card, before today). When the real entry shows up — typed, imported, or synced — finangui matches it to a projected occurrence by amount and by a nearby date, and confirms that occurrence instead of creating a separate one.

## Overdue occurrences

A projected occurrence whose date has passed with no match shows up as overdue. The **Pendências** (Pending items) card on Início surfaces these, letting you mark each as **Aconteceu** (It happened — confirms it, details adjustable) or **Não aconteceu** (Didn't happen — skips it).

## Editing and skipping

Simple field changes only apply to occurrences dated today or later. Changing the schedule itself, or pausing the recurrence, is more disruptive: it clears recent projected occurrences and regenerates them. Marking an occurrence **Não aconteceu** skips that date.

<Callout type="info">
  A skipped date is remembered forever — even after a later schedule change, pause, or reactivation, it's never generated again.
</Callout>

## Categorization

Projected occurrences are excluded from automatic categorization. One only picks up a category once confirmed as a real entry, through the same sources described in [Categorization](/docs/features/categorization).

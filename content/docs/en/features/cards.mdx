title: Cards
description: How finangui tracks credit card statements, payments, installment purchases and the limit you have left to spend.

A **Cartão de crédito** (credit card) is an account like any other — manual or linked through [Banking](/docs/features/banking) — with the same full-ledger balance rule as [Accounts](/docs/features/accounts): no "as of today" cutoff.

![A card statement](/screenshots/cards.png)

## Statements open, close and come due on their own

- Every purchase is assigned to a statement automatically, on create or edit — never picked by hand.
- Connected card: finangui rechecks the closing/due day on every sync, using the most common day among the bank's 3 latest statements.
- A statement already recorded keeps its own dates.
- **Editar datas** (edit dates): edit closing date, due date and reported total by hand — never moves a transaction to another statement.

## Total, reported total and computed total

- **Total da fatura**, **Pago** (paid), **Restante** (remaining): always computed, never typed in.
- Once closed, the bank's reported total (when there is one) is the headline number.
- finangui also computes its own total (outflows minus refunds, excluding payments) to compare.
- Status: **Aberta** (open), **Fechada** (closed), **Paga em parte** (partial), **Paga** (paid), or **Vencida** (overdue) past due, with a countdown like "Vence em 3 dias".

<Callout type="info">
  Totals disagree: a divergence warning. Cycle starts before the oldest synced transaction: a neutral "incomplete" note instead — Open Finance shares only ~12 months of history.
</Callout>

## Paying a statement is a transfer, never an expense

![The pay-statement dialog](/screenshots/cards-pay.png)

- **Pagar fatura**: always a transfer.
- Recognized as a payment when it's one leg of a transfer, matches a bank-reported payment, or matches a known payment description.
- Never reduces the total — counts only under **Pago**, with its own icon and **Pagamento** label.
- Duplicate bank-reported payment: auto-marked **Ignorada** (ignored).
- Any other credit, like a refund, reduces the total normally.
- Manually recognized or corrected payment: never undone by a later sync.

## Installment purchases

- Account, amount, date and type lock once an installment exists.
- Description and category can still change, only on installments not yet posted.
- Deleting any installment deletes the whole plan.
- Canceling a plan removes only the still-projected installments.

## Limit used and available

- Connected and synced: used/available limit come straight from the bank.
- Otherwise: calculated locally, from the registered limit and the card's transactions.
- Future installments not yet posted: shown separately as **Parcelas futuras**, never counted into used limit.

title: Cards

---

A **Cartão de crédito** (credit card) is an account — manual or [Banking](/docs/features/banking)-linked — with the full-ledger rule from [Accounts](/docs/features/accounts): no "as of today" cutoff.

![A card statement](/screenshots/cards.png)

## Statements manage their own dates

- Every purchase gets a statement automatically — never picked by hand.
- Connected: closing/due day rechecked each sync, from the 3 latest statements' most common day.
- A recorded statement never has its dates rewritten.
- **Editar datas** (edit dates): closing, due date and reported total, by hand — never moves a transaction.

## Reported total vs. computed total

- **Total da fatura**, **Pago** (paid), **Restante** (remaining): always computed, never typed in.
- Closed: bank's reported total, if any, is the headline number.
- finangui's own total (outflows minus refunds, no payments) is kept for comparison.
- Status: **Aberta** (open), **Fechada** (closed), **Paga em parte** (partial), **Paga** (paid), **Vencida** (overdue) — with a "Vence em 3 dias" countdown.

<Callout type="info">
  Totals disagree: a divergence warning. Cycle starts before the oldest synced transaction: a neutral "incomplete" note instead.
</Callout>

## Paying is a transfer, never an expense

![The pay-statement dialog](/screenshots/cards-pay.png)

- **Pagar fatura**: always a transfer.
- Recognized as a transfer leg, a bank-reported payment, or a known description match.
- Never reduces the total — counts only under **Pago**, with its own icon and **Pagamento** label.
- Duplicate bank-reported payment: auto-marked **Ignorada** (ignored).
- Any other credit (e.g. a refund): reduces the total normally.
- A manual fix is never undone by a later sync.

## Installment purchases

- Account, amount, date and type lock once an installment exists.
- Description and category: editable, only if not yet posted.
- Deleting any installment deletes the whole plan.
- Canceling removes only the still-projected installments.

## Limit used and available

- Connected and synced: used/available limit come straight from the bank.
- Otherwise: calculated from the registered limit and the card's transactions.
- Future unposted installments: shown separately as **Parcelas futuras**, never counted as used.

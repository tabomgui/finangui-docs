title: Cards

A **Cartão de crédito** (credit card) is an account like any other — manual or linked through [Banking](/docs/features/banking) — using the same full-ledger balance rule from [Accounts](/docs/features/accounts): no "as of today" cutoff. This page covers what's specific to cards: statements, payments, installments and limit usage.

![A card statement](/screenshots/cards.png)

## Statements open, close and come due on their own

Every card purchase is assigned to a statement automatically when you create or edit the transaction — you never pick one by hand. For a card connected through **Banking**, finangui re-checks the closing and due day on every sync, based on whichever day is most common among the bank's three most recent statements; a statement already recorded never gets its own dates rewritten. You can also edit a statement's closing date, due date and reported total by hand from **Editar datas** (edit dates); this never moves a transaction to a different statement.

## Total, reported total and computed total

A statement's **Total da fatura**, **Pago** (paid) and **Restante** (remaining) are always computed, never typed in directly. Once closed, if the bank reports a total, that's the headline number; finangui also keeps its own computed total (outflows minus refunds, excluding payments) to compare against it. Status badges are **Aberta** (open), **Fechada** (closed), **Paga em parte** (partial) and **Paga** (paid) — or **Vencida** (overdue) once the due date passes unpaid, with a countdown like "Vence em 3 dias" next to it.

<Callout type="info">
  When the two totals disagree, a divergence warning compares them. If the statement's cycle starts before the oldest transaction finangui has synced for that account, that warning is replaced by a neutral "incomplete" note instead — Open Finance typically shares only about 12 months of history.
</Callout>

## Paying a statement is a transfer, never an expense

Paying a card statement (**Pagar fatura**) always registers as a transfer, never an expense. finangui recognizes a credit as a payment when it's one leg of a transfer, matches a payment the bank reported, or matches a known bank payment description — it never reduces the total, counting only under **Pago**, with its own icon and the **Pagamento** label. A duplicate bank-reported payment is auto-marked **Ignorada** (ignored). Any other credit, like a refund, reduces the total normally. A manually recognized or corrected payment is never undone by a later sync.

## Installment purchases

Each installment's account, amount, date and type lock once it exists; description and category can still change, but only on installments not yet posted. Deleting any single installment deletes the whole plan; canceling a plan only removes the still-projected installments.

## Limit used and available

Used and available limit come straight from the bank when the card is connected and synced; otherwise finangui calculates them locally from the registered limit and the card's transactions. Future installments not yet posted show separately as **Parcelas futuras**, never counted into the used limit.

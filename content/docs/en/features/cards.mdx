title: Cards
description: How finangui tracks credit card statements, payments, installment purchases and the limit you have left to spend.
---

A **Cartão de crédito** (credit card) is an account — manual or [Banking](/docs/features/banking)-linked — with the full-ledger rule from [Accounts](/docs/features/accounts): no "as of today" cutoff.

![A card statement](/screenshots/cards.png)

## Statements manage their own dates

- Every purchase lands on a statement automatically, never by hand.
- Connected card: closing and due day are rechecked on each sync.
- A recorded statement never has its dates rewritten.
- **Editar datas** (edit dates): adjusts closing, due date and reported total by hand, without moving transactions.

## Reported total vs. computed total

- **Total da fatura**, **Pago** (paid), **Restante** (remaining): always computed, never typed in.
- Closed statement: the bank's reported total, if any, is the headline; finangui computes its own (outflows minus refunds, no payments) to compare.
- Status: **Aberta** (open), **Fechada** (closed), **Paga em parte** (partial), **Paga** (paid), **Vencida** (overdue) — with a "Vence em 3 dias" countdown.

<Callout type="info">
  Totals disagree: a divergence warning. Cycle starts before the oldest synced transaction: a neutral "incomplete" note instead.
</Callout>

## Paying is a transfer, never an expense

![The pay-statement dialog](/screenshots/cards-pay.png)

- **Pagar fatura** always records a transfer.
- Never reduces the total: counts only under **Pago**, with the **Pagamento** label.
- Duplicate bank-reported payment: auto-marked **Ignorada** (ignored).
- Other credit (e.g. a refund): reduces the total normally.
- A manual fix is never undone by a sync.

## Installment purchases

- Account, amount, date and type lock once the installment exists.
- Description and category: editable only if not yet posted.
- Deleting an installment deletes the whole plan; canceling removes only the still-projected ones.

## Limit used and available

- Connected and synced: used and available come from the bank.
- Otherwise: calculated from the registered limit and transactions.
- Unposted future installments show separately as **Parcelas futuras**, outside the used limit.

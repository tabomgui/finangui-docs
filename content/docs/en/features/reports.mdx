title: Reports
description: How finangui's Relatórios screen shows monthly income-versus-expense evolution and spending compared between two periods.
---

**Relatórios** (Reports) has two reports: **Evolução mensal** (Monthly evolution), income versus expense over time, and **Comparação por categoria** (Category comparison), spending by category between two periods you pick. Both exclude transfers between your own accounts and use your main currency.

![The Relatórios screen](/screenshots/reports.png)

## Monthly evolution

Plots income and expense side by side for each month in the range. A dropdown picks the range: **Últimos 6 meses** (Last 6 months), **Últimos 12 meses** (Last 12 months), or **Ano atual** (Current year).

## Category comparison

Compares spending per category across two independently-steppable months, **Período A** and **Período B**, plus a total row. The difference (always B minus A) is highlighted — a bigger spend in B in expense tone, a smaller one in income tone.

A subcategory's spending rolls up into its root category; anything uncategorized gets its own **Sem categoria** (No category) row.

## Purchase date or statement due date

A toggle above both reports controls which date a transaction is grouped by: **Data da compra** (Purchase date) or **Vencimento da fatura** (Statement due date).

<Callout type="info">
  Only credit card purchases are affected. **Vencimento da fatura** moves a card purchase to the month its linked statement is due, which can differ from the purchase month. Every other transaction stays on its own date. See [Cards](/docs/features/cards) for how a purchase gets attached to a statement.
</Callout>

## Related to the Início spending breakdown

The **Distribuição de gastos** (Spending breakdown) card on **Início** (Home) is built on the same logic: one entry per subcategory with spending, plus a combined entry when the parent category also has direct spending. It's a more compact view of the current month, but the numbers match Relatórios.

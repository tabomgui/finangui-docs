**Relatórios** (Reports): two reports, excluding transfers between your accounts, in your main currency.

- **Evolução mensal** (Monthly evolution) — income vs. expense over time.
- **Comparação por categoria** (Category comparison) — spending by category, two periods.

![The Relatórios screen](/screenshots/reports.png)

## Monthly evolution

- Income and expense, side by side, per month.
- Range: **Últimos 6 meses**, **Últimos 12 meses**, or **Ano atual**.

## Category comparison

- **Período A** vs. **Período B** (each steppable), plus a total row.
- Difference (B minus A) highlighted — expense tone if bigger, income tone if smaller.
- A subcategory rolls into its root; uncategorized gets its own **Sem categoria** row.

## Purchase date or statement due date

A toggle groups by **Data da compra** (Purchase date) or **Vencimento da fatura** (Statement due date).

<Callout type="info">
  Card purchases only — due date moves them to the statement's month. See [Cards](/docs/features/cards).
</Callout>

## Related to the Início spending breakdown

**Distribuição de gastos** (Spending breakdown), on **Início**, uses the same logic: one entry per subcategory, plus a combined entry if the parent also spends directly. Same numbers, more compact, current month only.

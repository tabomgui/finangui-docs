---
title: Transfers
description: How finangui detects, suggests, links, and unlinks transfers between your own accounts.
---

A transfer is money moving between two of your own accounts — an outflow from one and an inflow into another, linked as the two legs of the same movement. Linked legs never get their own category, and are never touched by categorization rules or history.

![A transfer suggestion](/screenshots/transfers.png)

## Automatic detection

finangui looks for transfers among just-added entries after every import and bank sync, comparing candidates within 2 days of each other. You can also search the last 90 days anytime from **Transferências encontradas** (Transfers found) (`/transferencias/sugestoes`), via **Procurar agora** (Search now).

Candidate pairs are scored by date proximity, a clue in the description or account name, and whether the inflow landed on a credit card. A uniquely clear match links automatically; anything less certain becomes a suggestion (at most two per entry) waiting for your confirmation.

## Reviewing suggestions

Pending suggestions show up on the **Transferências encontradas** page, linked from the **Pendências** (Pending) card on Início whenever one is waiting. Each shows both legs side by side.

- **Juntar** (Join) accepts the suggestion and links the two legs.
- **Não é transferência** (Not a transfer) dismisses it; both entries stay as regular entries.

## Linking two entries by hand

If finangui didn't detect or suggest anything, select two entries from the transactions list and use **Juntar como transferência** (Join as transfer) — finangui figures out which is the outflow and which the inflow on its own.

## Unlinking a transfer

**Desfazer transferência** (Undo transfer), from either leg's detail screen, splits the two legs apart into regular, uncategorized entries.

<Callout type="info">
  An explicit undo is remembered, so detection won't re-link that pair. An automatic unlink (reverted import, cleaned-up pending bank entries) instead tries to recategorize the leg that lost its category, same as a newly imported entry.
</Callout>

## Deleting a leg that came from your bank

Deleting one leg of a transfer never deletes both: finangui unlinks the pair first, so the surviving leg becomes a regular entry, then deletes the one you asked to remove. The same applies when reverting an import batch or cleaning up old pending bank entries.

See also [Imports](/docs/features/imports) and [Banking](/docs/features/banking), which both trigger transfer detection.

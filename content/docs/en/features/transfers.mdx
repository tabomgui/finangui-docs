---
title: Transfers
description: How finangui detects, suggests, links, and unlinks transfers between your own accounts.
---

A transfer is money moving between two of your own accounts — an outflow and an inflow linked as one movement. Linked legs never get a category, rule, or history match.

![A transfer suggestion](/screenshots/transfers.png)

## Automatic detection

- Runs after every import and bank sync, on entries within 2 days of each other.
- Also runs on demand, over the last 90 days, from **Transferências encontradas** (Transfers found) (`/transferencias/sugestoes`) via **Procurar agora** (Search now).
- Scored by date proximity, a clue in description/account name, and a credit-card inflow.
- A uniquely clear match links automatically; weaker ones become a suggestion (max two per entry).

## Reviewing suggestions

Suggestions show on **Transferências encontradas**, linked from the **Pendências** (Pending) card on Início.

- **Juntar** (Join) — links the two legs.
- **Não é transferência** (Not a transfer) — dismisses it; both stay regular.

## Linking two entries by hand

Select two entries and use **Juntar como transferência** (Join as transfer) — finangui figures out the outflow and inflow.

## Unlinking a transfer

**Desfazer transferência** (Undo transfer), from either leg's detail screen, splits the legs back into regular, uncategorized entries.

<Callout type="info">
  An explicit undo is remembered, so detection won't re-link that pair. An automatic unlink instead tries to recategorize the leg that lost its category.
</Callout>

## Deleting a leg that came from your bank

Deleting one leg never deletes both: finangui unlinks the pair first, then deletes the one you asked for. Same when reverting an import or cleaning up pending bank entries.

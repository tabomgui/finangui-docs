---
title: Transfers
description: How finangui detects, suggests, links, and unlinks transfers between your own accounts.
---

A transfer is money moving between your own accounts — linked outflow and inflow legs. Linked legs never get a category, rule, or history match.

## Automatic detection

- Runs after every import and sync, on entries within 2 days of each other.
- Also runs on demand, over the last 90 days, from **Transferências encontradas** (Transfers found) via **Procurar agora** (Search now).
- Scored by date proximity, a clue in description/account name, and a credit-card inflow.
- A uniquely clear match links automatically; weaker ones become a suggestion (max two per entry).

## Reviewing suggestions

Suggestions show on **Transferências encontradas**, via the **Pendências** (Pending) card on Início.

- **Juntar** (Join) — links the two legs.
- **Não é transferência** (Not a transfer) — dismisses it; both stay regular.

## Linking two entries by hand

<Steps>
<Step>
On **Transações**, click **Selecionar** (Select) and check the two entries.
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Juntar como transferência** (Join as transfer): finangui figures out the outflow and inflow.

![Two entries selected](/screenshots/transfers-join.png)
</Step>
</Steps>

## Unlinking a transfer

**Desfazer transferência** (Undo transfer), from either leg's detail, splits the legs into regular, uncategorized entries.

<Callout type="info">
  An explicit undo blocks re-detection of that pair. An automatic unlink instead tries to recategorize the leg that lost its category.
</Callout>

## Deleting a leg that came from your bank

Deleting a leg never deletes both: finangui unlinks the pair, then deletes the one you asked for — same for reverting an import or clearing pending bank entries.

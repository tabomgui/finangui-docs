---
title: Goals
description: How finangui tracks progress toward a savings target, either from an account's balance or from manual contributions.
---

A **Meta** (goal) is a savings target: a name, a target amount, and optionally a target date. On the **Metas** (Goals) screen, **Nova meta** (New goal) opens a form to set one up.

## Two ways to track progress

A goal tracks progress one of two ways, and you pick which one when you create it:

- **Linked to an account** — turning on **Acompanhar saldo de uma conta** (Track an account's balance) and choosing an [account](/docs/features/accounts) makes the goal's progress exactly that account's current balance. The form is explicit about the trade-off: progress replaces the sum of manual contributions.
- **Unlinked, funded by contributions** — leave that switch off and progress is instead the running total of **aportes** (contributions) you log by hand, through **Novo aporte** (New contribution) on the goal's card. A contribution can be a deposit or, toggling **Retirada** (Withdrawal) in the same form, money taken back out.

These two modes are mutually exclusive: a goal linked to an account never accepts a manual contribution.

## Percent and remaining

Each goal shows progress as a percentage and as a remaining amount — progress so far against the target amount.

<Callout type="info">
  When a linked account's balance is negative, the percentage floors at 0% instead of going negative — but the remaining amount still reflects the real gap, including that negative balance. It is not clipped to match the 0% shown.
</Callout>

## Target date and monthly pace

A goal can optionally carry a target date. When it does, and the goal isn't achieved yet, finangui shows how much to set aside each month to reach the target amount by that date.

## Reaching the goal

<Callout type="info">
  A goal is marked achieved the first time its progress reaches the target amount — the card picks up an **Atingida** (Achieved) badge. That mark sticks: if progress later dips back below the target (an account balance drops, or a withdrawal is logged), the goal stays marked achieved. It isn't a status recalculated live on every view.

  The only way to undo it is editing the goal and raising its target amount to a value current progress no longer reaches — at that point the goal goes back to not achieved.
</Callout>

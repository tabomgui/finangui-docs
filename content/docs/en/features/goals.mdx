A **Meta** (goal) is a savings target: name, target amount, optional target date. **Nova meta** (New goal) opens the form.

![The Metas screen](/screenshots/goals.png)

## Two ways to track progress

![Creating a goal](/screenshots/goal-form.png)

Pick one, mutually exclusive:

- **Linked to an account** — **Acompanhar saldo de uma conta** (Track an account's balance) makes progress that [account](/docs/features/accounts)'s balance.
- **Unlinked** — progress is the total of **aportes** (contributions) via **Novo aporte** (New contribution): deposits, or **Retirada** (Withdrawal) for money out.

## Progress and pace

- Percentage and remaining amount.
- A negative linked balance floors the percentage at 0%, not the remaining amount.
- An optional target date shows how much to set aside monthly.

## Reaching the goal

<Callout type="info">
  **Atingida** (Achieved) marks the first time progress reaches the target, and sticks even after it dips. Only raising the target past current progress undoes it.
</Callout>

title: Notifications

Bell icon: what needs attention — sheet on mobile, popover on desktop. Click opens the screen.

![The notifications bell](/screenshots/notifications.png)

The bell sits at the bottom of the sidebar. **Marcar todas como lidas** (Mark all as read) marks everything as seen.

## The four kinds of alert

- **Fatura vencendo** (Invoice due) — statement due soon ([Cards](/docs/features/cards)).
- **Orçamento estourado** (Budget exceeded) — category over its limit ([Budgets](/docs/features/budgets)).
- **Lançamentos pendentes** (Pending entries) — overdue, unconfirmed recurrence ([Recurrences](/docs/features/recurrences)).
- **Reconexão necessária** (Reconnection needed) — needs fresh credentials ([Banking](/docs/features/banking)).

<Callout type="info">
  First three: once daily, one summary each. Reconnection fires immediately, mid-sync.
</Callout>

## Deduplication and cleanup

- The same alert never repeats.
- Read notifications older than 90 days are deleted automatically.

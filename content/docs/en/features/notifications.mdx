The bell icon surfaces things finangui thinks you should know about — a sheet on mobile, a popover on desktop — and a click takes you to the relevant screen.

## The four kinds of alert

- **Fatura vencendo** (Invoice due) — a card statement nearing its due date. See [Cards](/docs/features/cards).
- **Orçamento estourado** (Budget exceeded) — a category over its monthly limit. See [Budgets](/docs/features/budgets).
- **Lançamentos pendentes** (Pending entries) — an overdue, unconfirmed recurring entry. See [Recurrences](/docs/features/recurrences).
- **Reconexão necessária** (Reconnection needed) — a connection needs fresh credentials. See [Banking](/docs/features/banking).

<Callout type="info">
  The first three are checked once a day, one summary per kind. Reconnection fires immediately, during a sync.
</Callout>

## Deduplication and cleanup

finangui never sends the same alert twice for the same thing. Read notifications older than 90 days are deleted automatically.

title: Notifications
description: How finangui alerts you about invoices, budgets, overdue recurrences, and bank connections that need attention.

The bell icon keeps you aware of a handful of things finangui thinks you should know about, without you having to go check each screen yourself. On mobile it sits in the header and opens as a sheet from the bottom; on desktop it sits in the sidebar and opens as a popover. Either way, clicking a notification takes you straight to the relevant screen.

## The four kinds of alert

- **Fatura vencendo** (Invoice due) — a credit card statement is approaching its due date. See [Cards](/docs/features/cards).
- **Orçamento estourado** (Budget exceeded) — spending in a category has gone over its monthly limit. See [Budgets](/docs/features/budgets).
- **Lançamentos pendentes** (Pending entries) — a recurring entry's projected occurrence is past its date and still hasn't been confirmed. See [Recurrences](/docs/features/recurrences).
- **Reconexão necessária** (Reconnection needed) — finangui noticed a connection needs your credentials refreshed before it can keep syncing. See [Banking](/docs/features/banking).

<Callout type="info">
  The first three alerts are checked once a day and arrive as a single daily summary per kind — never one notification per invoice, per category, or per occurrence. The reconnection alert is different: it fires immediately, the moment finangui notices during a sync, rather than waiting for the next day's check.
</Callout>

## No duplicate alerts

finangui never sends the same alert twice for the same underlying thing. Once you've been notified about a given invoice, budget, overdue entry, or connection issue, you won't get a second, identical notification piling up about it.

## Notifications don't pile up forever

Read notifications older than 90 days are cleaned up automatically, so the list stays relevant instead of growing without limit.

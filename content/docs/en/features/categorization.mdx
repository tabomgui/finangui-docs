title: Categorization
description: How transactions get a category automatically, and how the Categorizar screen clears a backlog of uncategorized entries in bulk.
---

Every transaction ends up with a category, but most never need a human to pick one by hand. finangui tries, in order: a matching **rule** (a condition on amount always looks at the installment's own amount, never the purchase total), then a **history-based suggestion** when no rule matches, then the bank's own reported category, matched by name — only for transactions synced through [Banking](/docs/features/banking). Whichever source decides it (or a manual choice) is recorded in `categorized_by`: `manual`, `history`, `pluggy`, or `rule:{id}`. Transfer legs are never touched by rules or history.

Whatever none of these resolve piles up on the **Categorizar** (Categorize) screen — linked from the **Pendências** (Pending items) card on Início and from Transações whenever there are pending entries.

![The Categorizar screen](/screenshots/categorize.png)

## What lands on Categorizar

`/categorizar` clears, all at once, the uncategorized entries left after an import or bank sync — already-posted entries from every account, credit cards included. It never includes projected entries, transfers, ignored entries, statement payments, or entries on archived accounts.

## Grouped by counterparty

Entries are grouped by counterparty: finangui strips the bank description's leading transaction-type prefix (e.g. "Pix enviado") and a trailing city-and-country suffix, and groups by what's left — so a Pix sent and one received from the same person land in the same group. Each group shows as a card with its outflow/inflow counts and net total.

## Selecting and applying

A group's header checkbox selects everything in it; opening a group lets you check entries individually. On a group mixing both directions, **só saídas**/**só entradas** select just one way, and several groups can be selected at once. A same-direction group with strong-enough history shows a one-click suggestion.

<Steps>
<Step>
With entries selected, the footer action bar shows a category selector — inflow or outflow only, matching the direction of your selection.
</Step>
<Step>
To create a new category, type its name and choose **Criar…** (Create…), optionally **Dentro de** (Inside) an existing one as a subcategory.
</Step>
<Step>
Confirm. The selected entries get the category right away.
</Step>
</Steps>

## The rule created on apply

By default, applying a category also creates a rule so future similar entries get categorized the same way — one alternative per checked group, shaped as "original description contains `<group name>`" AND "type equals" your selection's direction. The rule is named "`<group>` → `<category>`" and shows up afterward on **Regras** (Rules). The block also shows how many other pending entries the rule already matches, with a checkbox to apply it to those too, synchronously in the same transaction as the category apply.

![The Regras screen](/screenshots/rules.png)

<Callout type="info">
  The rule toggle defaults off when you only checked part of a group, and becomes unavailable past 19 selected groups (the backend caps a rule at 20 conditions total).
</Callout>

If the default shape isn't what you want, **Personalizar** (Customize) opens a full rule editor instead; **Voltar ao padrão** (Back to default) discards it.

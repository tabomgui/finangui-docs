---
title: Categorization
description: How transactions get a category automatically, and how the Categorizar screen clears a backlog of uncategorized entries in bulk.
---

Every transaction ends up with a category. finangui tries, in order:

1. A matching **rule** — an amount condition looks at the installment's own amount, never the purchase total.
2. A **history-based suggestion**, when no rule matches.
3. The bank's reported category, matched by name — only for [Banking](/docs/features/banking)-synced transactions.

Recorded in `categorized_by`: `manual`, `history`, `pluggy`, or `rule:{id}`. Transfer legs are never touched by rules or history.

Unresolved entries land on **Categorizar** (Categorize) — linked from the **Pendências** card on Início and from Transações.

![The Categorizar screen](/screenshots/categorize.png)

## What lands on Categorizar

- Already-posted, uncategorized entries from every account, cards included.
- Never: projected entries, transfers, ignored entries, statement payments, archived-account entries.

## Grouped by counterparty

- Strips the bank's leading transaction-type prefix (e.g. "Pix enviado") and a trailing city/country suffix.
- Groups by what's left — a Pix sent and one received from the same person land together.
- Each group card shows outflow/inflow counts and net total.

## Selecting and applying

- Header checkbox selects the whole group; opening it lets you check entries individually.
- Mixed-direction group: **só saídas**/**só entradas** select just one side.
- Multiple groups selectable at once.
- Strong-enough history on a same-direction group: one-click suggestion.

![The footer action bar after selecting a group](/screenshots/categorize-apply.png)

<Steps>
<Step>
Footer action bar shows a category selector matching your selection's direction.
</Step>
<Step>
To create a category: type a name, choose **Criar…** (Create…), optionally **Dentro de** (Inside) an existing one.
</Step>
<Step>
Confirm — selected entries get the category right away.
</Step>
</Steps>

## The rule created on apply

- Applying a category creates a rule by default, for future similar entries.
- Shape: one alternative per checked group — "original description contains `<group name>`" AND "type equals" your selection's direction.
- Named "`<group>` → `<category>`"; shows up on **Regras** (Rules).
- Shows how many other pending entries it already matches, with a checkbox to apply it there too — synchronous, same transaction.

![The Regras screen](/screenshots/rules.png)

<Callout type="info">
  Defaults off for a partially checked group; disables past 19 selected groups (backend caps a rule at 20 conditions).
</Callout>

**Personalizar** (Customize) opens a full rule editor instead; **Voltar ao padrão** (Back to default) discards it.

---
title: Categorization
description: How transactions get a category automatically, and how the Categorizar screen clears a backlog of uncategorized entries in bulk.
---

Every transaction gets a category. finangui tries, in order:

1. A matching **rule** — an amount condition looks at the installment amount, never the purchase total.
2. A **history-based suggestion**, when no rule matches.
3. The bank's reported category, matched by name — only for [Banking](/docs/features/banking)-synced transactions.

Recorded in `categorized_by`: `manual`, `history`, `pluggy`, or `rule:{id}`. Transfer legs: never touched.

Unresolved entries land on **Categorizar** (Categorize) — linked from **Pendências** on Início and from Transações.

![The Categorizar screen](/screenshots/categorize.png)

## What lands here

- Already-posted, uncategorized entries from every account, cards included.
- Never: projected, transfers, ignored entries, statement payments, archived-account entries.

## Grouped by counterparty

- Strips the bank's transaction-type prefix (e.g. "Pix enviado") and trailing city/country suffix.
- Groups by what's left — a Pix sent and received from the same person land together.
- Each group card shows outflow/inflow counts and net total.

## Selecting and applying

- Header checkbox selects the whole group; open it to check entries individually.
- Mixed-direction group: **só saídas**/**só entradas** select just one side.
- Multiple groups selectable at once.
- Strong-enough history on a same-direction group: one-click suggestion.

![The footer action bar after selecting a group](/screenshots/categorize-apply.png)

<Steps>
<Step>
Footer bar shows a category selector matching your direction.
</Step>
<Step>
To create a category: type a name, choose **Criar…** (Create…), optionally **Dentro de** (Inside) an existing one.
</Step>
<Step>
Confirm — selected entries get the category right away.
</Step>
</Steps>

## The rule created

- Applying a category creates a rule by default, for similar future entries.
- One alternative per checked group: "description contains `<group name>`" AND "type equals" your direction.
- Named "`<group>` → `<category>`"; shows up on **Regras** (Rules).
- Shows how many pending entries it matches, with a checkbox to apply there too, same transaction.

![The Regras screen](/screenshots/rules.png)

<Callout type="info">
  Off by default for a partial group; disabled past 19 groups (rule cap: 20 conditions).
</Callout>

**Personalizar** (Customize) opens a full rule editor instead; **Voltar ao padrão** (Back to default) discards it.

![The rule editor](/screenshots/rule-editor.png)

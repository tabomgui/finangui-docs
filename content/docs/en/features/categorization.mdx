---
title: Categorization
description: How transactions get a category automatically, and how the Categorizar screen clears a backlog of uncategorized entries in bulk.
---

Every transaction gets a category. finangui tries, in order:

1. A matching **rule** — an amount condition looks at the installment amount, never the purchase total.
2. A **history-based suggestion**, when no rule matches.
3. The bank's reported category, matched by name — only for [Banking](/docs/features/banking)-synced transactions.

Transfer legs are never touched.

Unresolved entries land on **Categorizar** (Categorize) — linked from **Pendências** on Início and from Transações.

![The Categorizar screen](/screenshots/categorize.png)

## What lands here

- Posted, uncategorized entries from every account, cards included.
- Never: projected, transfers, ignored entries, statement payments, archived-account entries.

Entries are grouped by counterparty, without the transaction-type prefix ("Pix enviado") or the city/country suffix. A Pix sent and received from the same person land together. Each card shows outflow/inflow counts and net total.

## Selecting and applying

- The header checkbox selects the group; open it to check entries individually.
- Mixed-direction group: **só saídas**/**só entradas** select one side.
- You can select several groups at once.
- Same-direction group with strong history: one-click suggestion.

![The footer action bar after selecting a group](/screenshots/categorize-apply.png)

<Steps>
<Step>
In the footer action bar, pick the category.
</Step>
<Step>
For a new category: type a name and choose **Criar…** (Create…); optionally **Dentro de** (Inside) an existing one.
</Step>
<Step>
Confirm: the entries get the category right away.
</Step>
</Steps>

## The rule created

Applying a category also creates, by default, a rule for similar future entries.

- Condition: "description contains `<group name>`" AND "type equals" the direction, one alternative per checked group.
- Named "`<group>` → `<category>`"; shows up on **Regras** (Rules).
- Shows how many pending entries it matches, with a checkbox to apply to them too.

![The Regras screen](/screenshots/rules.png)

<Callout type="info">
  Off by default for a partial group; disabled past 19 groups (rule cap: 20 conditions).
</Callout>

**Personalizar** (Customize) opens a full rule editor instead; **Voltar ao padrão** (Back to default) discards it.

![The rule editor](/screenshots/rule-editor.png)
